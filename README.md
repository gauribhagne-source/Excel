# 🛒 Flipkart Product Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to analyze Flipkart product data and provide interactive insights into **product volume, pricing, discounts, price tiers, brands, categories, and customer ratings** to support data-driven product and pricing analysis.

The dashboard transforms raw Flipkart product data into meaningful visual insights using Microsoft Excel.

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains product-level information from Flipkart. Each row represents a product and includes information related to the product name, brand, category, retail price, discounted price, discount percentage, and customer rating.

### Dataset Columns

| **Column**       | **Description**                                 |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Product Name     | Name of the product                             |
| Brand            | Brand associated with the product               |
| Category         | Product category                                |
| Retail Price     | Original price of the product                   |
| Discounted Price | Price after discount                            |
| Discount         | Discount percentage offered                     |
| Rating           | Customer rating of the product                  |
| Price Tier       | Classification of products based on price range |

---

## 🧮 Calculated Columns

* **Discount** – Represents the percentage discount offered on the product.
* **Price Tier** – Classifies products into different price ranges based on their price.
* **Discounted Price** – Represents the selling price after applying the discount.

# 📝 Conclusion

The Flipkart Product Analysis Dashboard provides a consolidated view of **product availability, pricing, discounts, price tiers, and customer ratings**.

The analysis shows that the dataset contains **20,000 products**, with an average retail price of **₹22,648** and an average discounted price of **₹16,662**. The average discount across products is **43.7%**, while the average product rating is **4.18**.

**Clothing** has the highest product volume among the categories analyzed, while **Computers** has the lowest. The **Mid-Range** price tier contains the highest number of products, showing that a significant portion of the product catalog falls within this price segment.

The rating analysis shows an average rating of **4.18**, with **5.00 being the most common rating level**.

Overall, the dashboard helps identify **category-level product volume, pricing patterns, discount levels, price segmentation, and rating trends**, making it easier to understand the Flipkart product marketplace and support data-driven analysis.
